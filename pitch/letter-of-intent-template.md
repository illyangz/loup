**Re: Pilot of the Loup employee benefits platform**

To whom it may concern,

[Institution] confirms its interest in a **90-day pilot** of the Loup employee benefits platform for its staff, subject to final agreement on terms.

**Proposed pilot scope:**

1. **Employee base:** approximately \_\_\_\_\_\_ eligible employees (target 20–50) across \_\_\_\_\_\_ campus(es).
2. **Benefit allowance:** a monthly allowance per employee (e.g., AED 400–750/month), funded by the institution, structured by staff tier.
3. **Services:** institution to confirm service categories of interest (household & life admin, personal wellbeing, fitness & recovery, mobility & convenience, family & dependent support, personal development, recreation & lifestyle).
4. **Fees during pilot:** waived — Loup provides the platform and coordinates provider fulfilment at no charge for the 90-day pilot.
5. **Roles:** the institution will designate a pilot administrator; Loup will provide onboarding, roster import, and a monthly utilization and spend report.
6. **Data:** pilot data is confidential to the institution; no cross-tenant data sharing.

**What the institution will evaluate:**

- Staff adoption and utilization of the benefit.
- Quality and reliability of provider fulfilment.
- Clarity and accuracy of spend and fee reporting.
- Readiness for a paid rollout (hybrid fee: small per-employee monthly fee + % of benefit spend, both configurable).
