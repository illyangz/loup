# 1. Product Definition

Loup is a UAE-first B2B2C flexible lifestyle-benefits and service-orchestration platform.

Its initial target market is educational institutions in Dubai, including:

* Schools and school groups
* Universities and university branch campuses
* Colleges
* Nurseries and early-learning groups
* Vocational institutions
* Professional-training institutions
* Education-management companies

Educational institutions allocate a configurable, non-cash service allowance to eligible employees. Employees use the allowance across an approved and expandable service ecosystem according to their individual needs.

If a service costs more than the available employer contribution, the employee can pay the difference personally.

Loup manages:

* Institutional eligibility rules
* Employee allowances
* Service discovery
* Booking
* Split payments
* Provider fulfilment
* Quality monitoring
* Customer support
* Privacy-safe employer reporting
* API-ready integration

Loup is not:

* A coupon or discount marketplace
* A bank
* A withdrawable financial wallet
* A medical or mental-health platform
* A consumer-only home-services application
* A peer-to-peer credit marketplace
* A replacement for an institution’s HR system

The core proposition is:

“Loup gives institutions controlled flexibility and gives employees benefits that adapt to their lives.”

# 2. MVP Objective

The MVP must demonstrate one complete transaction:

1. An educational institution creates a benefit plan.
2. The institution adds an employee to the plan.
3. The employee receives a monthly allowance.
4. The employee selects an approved service.
5. Loup calculates the employer contribution and employee top-up.
6. The employee confirms the booking.
7. A vendor receives and fulfils the order.
8. The benefit ledger is updated.
9. The institution sees aggregate, privacy-safe utilization.
10. Loup operations can monitor the transaction and provider quality.

Every important button in this flow must work. Avoid decorative features with no working behaviour.

* Allow the employee to change allocations
* Treat allocations as preferences, not locked financial transfers

## Three-step checkout

* Selected service
* Provider
* Date and time

### Step 2: Funding

Original service price
− Negotiated institutional saving

### Step 3: Confirmation

* Full payment summary

* Display name
* Relationship
* Service-access permission

Do not make household access phone-number based. The primary employee creates and manages the users they are authorized to support.

# 9. Benefit Ledger

Create an append-only allowance ledger.

The ledger must support:

* Allowance issuance
* Booking authorization
* Redemption
* Release of authorization
* Cancellation reversal
* Refund
* Manual adjustment by authorized Loup operations
* Allowance expiration
* Rollover, where institution policy permits

Never update balances by directly overwriting one balance field without recording a ledger transaction.

Each ledger entry should include:

* Unique ID
* Employee ID
* Institution ID
* Benefit-plan ID
* Transaction type
* Amount
* Booking reference
* Timestamp
* Expiration date
* Status
* Idempotency key
* Created-by role

The displayed balance must be derived or reconciled from valid ledger entries.

Prevent:

* Negative balances
* Duplicate redemption
* Cross-institution access
* Redemption of expired allowances
* Use of disallowed categories
